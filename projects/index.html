STRUCTURE · ML

Protein Surface Affinity

Teaching models to read molecular shape—and checking what they actually read.

PythonPyTorch GeometricProtein surfacesAMBERSLURM
Unfold the project story

Question

Can local protein-surface representations capture interaction and perturbation signals better than graph-based alternatives?

Approach

A MaSIF-inspired surface-patch workflow, a GNN comparator, matched wild-type–variant analysis, and molecular modelling with AMBER/SLURM.

My contribution

I developed the comparison workflow, linked mutation and PTM perturbations to local structure, and treated interpretation as part of evaluation.

What it changed

The project shifted my focus from headline performance to representation, matched comparisons and mechanistic evidence.
