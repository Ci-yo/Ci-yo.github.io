THE LONGER VERSION

Research, plotted as a trail.

This is not a perfectly straight specialisation story. It is a sequence of questions that kept borrowing tools from one another until a direction emerged.

  1. 01
    START

    Bioinformatics foundations

    Genomics, statistics, and programming established the habit that still drives the work: make the analysis inspectable, then ask whether the result makes biological sense.

  2. ≈
    2024

    Global river methane

    My first research experience moved from cleaning heterogeneous observations to asking defensible statistical questions about environmental and hydrological drivers.

  3. ⌬
    2025

    Protein structure & surfaces

    A molecule stopped being just a row in a table. Geometry, chemistry, and interaction neighbourhoods became explicit inputs to computational models.

  4. ∿
    2025

    Protein surface & PPI modelling

    MaSIF-inspired surface learning led to a deeper question than “does it predict?”: what information did the representation actually preserve, and what did the model use?

  5. Δ
    NEXT

    Variants & PTMs

    Matched wild-type and perturbed structures reframed the problem as molecular change: how can local geometric and chemical edits alter interaction behaviour?

  6. t
    NEXT

    Molecular dynamics

    Static structures are only snapshots. Molecular dynamics provides an ensemble-level test bed for structural hypotheses about mutations and post-translational modifications.

  7. A⁶
    2026 · BRANCH

    Cross-technology m6A benchmarking

    A different biological domain sharpened the same methodological concern: a strong metric can still sit on a fragile evaluation protocol. Calibration, heterogeneous evidence, and fair comparison became part of the science.

  8. ↗
    YOU ARE HERE

    Reliable biomolecular ML

    The current direction brings the route back together: structure-aware representation learning, perturbation modelling, molecular evidence, and evaluation that supports biological interpretation rather than decorating it.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

What keeps the route coherent.

The theme is not a single molecule or model architecture. It is a preference for biological representations and evaluation protocols that make claims inspectable.

01

Represent the biology

Geometry, chemistry, perturbation, and experimental context should be represented at the scale of the biological question.

02

Compare fairly

A benchmark is useful only when the competing methods are being asked the same question under the same information constraints.

03

Keep claims inspectable

Reproducible code, explicit splits, matched comparisons, and negative results make both success and uncertainty easier to see.
