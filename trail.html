Research, plotted as a trail.

    Global river methane

    Learning to ask questions with environmental data.

  3. 2025

    Structural biology

    A molecule is not just a row in a table.

    Working with protein structure shifted my attention from sequence-level signals to geometry, chemistry and the local context around interactions.

  4. 2025

    Protein surface & PPI

    Can a model read the shape of an interface?

    I explored surface-patch representations for protein interaction modelling and compared them with graph-based alternatives—then kept asking what the model had actually learned.

    See the evidence →

  5. NEXT

    Mutation & PTM effects

    Tiny molecular edits; occasionally dramatic opinions.

    Matched wild-type and variant comparisons turned the problem into perturbation modelling: how do local geometric or chemical changes propagate into binding behaviour?

    See the evidence →

  6. NEXT

    Molecular dynamics

    Because proteins decline to hold still for the camera.

    Molecular dynamics provides a route from static structures to ensembles, giving mutation and PTM hypotheses a physically informed test bed.

    See the evidence →

  7. BRANCH

    m6A benchmarking

    A detour that made evaluation part of the science.

    Cross-technology prediction highlighted how easily a good score can hide a fragile conclusion. This branch sharpened my interest in fair comparison, uncertainty and heterogeneous evidence.

  8. YOU ARE HERE

    Reliable biomolecular ML

    Structure-aware models, evaluated like the claims matter.

    My current direction brings the route back together: representation learning for biomolecules, perturbation-aware modelling and evaluation that supports biological interpretation rather than decorating it.
